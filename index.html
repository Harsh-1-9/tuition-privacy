Tuition App: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The Tuition App is used by a tuition teacher and their students to manage classes: attendance, fees, homework, notes, tests, notices and timetables. This page explains what information the app stores, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

InformationWhy the app needs it
Student name, class, mobile number, and the email address of the Google account used to sign inTo create the student's profile and let them sign in with their Google account.
Parent or guardian mobile numberSo the teacher can contact the parent, for example about fee reminders.
Attendance, fee status, test marksTo show the student and the teacher their records.
Batch membershipTo show each student only the notices, notes, homework and timetable of their own batches.
Join code entered at sign-upTo confirm the student was invited by the teacher.

The app does not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising identifiers, and it shows no ads.

How it is used

Information is used only to run the tuition classes: logging in, showing records, sending notices, and letting the teacher manage batches, attendance and fees. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

Who can see it

  • The teacher can see the records of all students.
  • Each student can see only their own records and the content of the batches they belong to.
  • Google Firebase stores the data and handles Google sign-in on our behalf (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google processes it under its own Firebase privacy terms.

We do not share student information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Children

Many students are under 18. The app is used only by students whose teacher gave them a join code, and the parent's mobile number is stored so the family stays informed. A parent or guardian can ask for a child's account to be deleted at any time, using the steps below.

Security and blocked accounts

Data is stored in Google's Firebase and is protected by access rules, so a student cannot read another student's records. The teacher can block an account. A blocked student cannot log in, and their records are hidden from the teacher's screens until the teacher unblocks them.

How long data is kept

Records are kept while the student's account exists, so that attendance, fees and marks stay available. They are removed when the account is deleted as described below.

Delete your account and data

You can ask for your account and all your records to be deleted at any time.

  1. Open the app and go to My account.
  2. Tap Request account deletion and confirm.
  3. Your teacher receives the request and approves it.
  4. When it is approved, your profile, attendance, fee records, test results and batch memberships are permanently deleted, and you are logged out.

Cannot open the app? Email harshjangra19062007@gmail.com with the student's name and the Google email they use in the app, and we will pass the request to the teacher.

Deletion cannot be undone. After it, the same Google account can sign up again as a new student with a join code. Your Google account email may stay on the login list of Firebase Authentication for a while after deletion; it is no longer linked to any name or records.
