Tuition App: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026. Provided by Harsh Jangra ("we").

The Tuition App helps private tuition teachers run their classes: batches, attendance, fees, notices, timetables, homework, notes, tests, recorded lectures and student questions (doubts). Each teacher creates their own tuition in the app, and students join it with a join code given by that teacher. Different tuitions are kept completely separate: people in one tuition cannot see another tuition's data.

There are three kinds of users: the owner (the teacher who created the tuition), co-teachers (teachers the owner invites by email) and students. This page explains what information the app stores, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

InformationWhy the app needs it
Everyone: name and email address of the Google account used to sign in, and a Firebase user IDTo sign you in and know which tuition you belong to.
Students: name, class, own mobile number, parent or guardian's mobile number, and the join code entered at sign-upTo create the student's profile, let the teacher contact the family (for example about fees), and confirm the student was invited.

Owners only: staff records (name, optional phone number, role and salary)So the owner can manage staff pay. Visible only to the owner, not to co-teachers or students.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, microphone recordings or advertising identifiers. It shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. It does not browse your photo gallery: it receives a photo only when a student picks one to attach to a doubt.

How it is used

Information is used only to run the tuition classes: signing you in, showing the right tuition's records, sending notices and notifications, answering doubts, and letting teachers manage batches, attendance and fees. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising, tracking or profiling.

Who can see it

  • The owner and co-teachers of a tuition can see that tuition's students and records, including their doubts. Only the owner can see staff and salary records, add or remove co-teachers, change the join code, or delete the tuition.
  • Each student can see only their own profile, attendance, fees, marks and doubts, and the notices, notes, homework, tests, lectures and timetable of the batches they belong to. They cannot see other students' records.
  • Other tuitions cannot see this tuition's data.
  • Google Firebase stores the data and provides Google sign-in, notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and app verification (App Check). Data is sent over encrypted connections. Google processes it under its own Firebase privacy terms.
  • Cloudflare runs a small service that sends notifications. It receives the notification text and the IDs of the students to notify, and does not keep them.
  • YouTube: lecture videos shared by a teacher play through YouTube inside the app. YouTube may collect its own usage data under Google's privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp: when a teacher taps a contact button, WhatsApp opens with the parent's or student's number and a message ready to send. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent there.

We do not share information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Children

The app is meant for students aged 13 and above. Many students are under 18. A student can only join with a join code from their teacher, and the sign-up screen asks students under 18 to have a parent or guardian's permission. The parent's mobile number is stored so the family stays informed. A parent or guardian can ask to see or delete a child's information at any time, using the steps below.

Security and blocked accounts

Data is stored in Google's Firebase and is protected by access rules, so every person can reach only their own tuition's data, and a student cannot read another student's records. A teacher can block a student's account; a blocked student cannot use the tuition's content until the teacher unblocks them. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your Google account protected.

How long data is kept

Records are kept while the account and the tuition exist, so that attendance, fees, marks and doubts stay available. They are removed when a student's deletion request is approved, when a co-teacher leaves, or when the owner deletes the tuition, as described below. A phone's notification token is also removed when the student logs out.

Delete your account and data

You can ask for your account and your records to be deleted at any time. Deletion cannot be undone.

Students and parents

  1. Open the app and go to My account.
  2. Tap Request account deletion and confirm.
  3. Your tuition's teacher receives the request and reviews it.
  4. When it is approved, your profile, attendance, fee records, test results, doubts and attached photos, batch memberships and notification token are permanently deleted, and you are logged out.

Teachers and tuition owners

Open the app, tap Settings on the home screen, then:

  • Owner: tap Delete this tuition and type the tuition's name to confirm. The tuition and everything in it (batches, students, fees, notices, doubts, staff records, co-teacher access and the join code) is permanently deleted.
  • Co-teacher: tap Leave this tuition. Your access and your account record are removed. The tuition itself stays.

Cannot open the app? Email harshjangra19062007@gmail.com from the Google account you use in the app, with your name and your tuition's name, and we will pass the request to your tuition's owner. Parents and guardians can send the request in the same way.

After deletion, the same Google account can sign up again as a new user. Your Google account itself is not affected, and your email address may stay on the sign-in list of Firebase Authentication for a while after deletion; it is no longer linked to any name or records.
